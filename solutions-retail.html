What the screening does

Simple for staff, clear for customers.

Guided from start to finish

The screen tells the operator what to do at each step. No clinical training is assumed.

Plain-language results

Customers see a short, educational summary of what was observed, in everyday words.

Referral path

Where a clinician's opinion would help, the screening says so and suggests the next step.

Optional product information

Relevant products can be shown in a separate, clearly labelled section. Never mixed in with the findings.

Event and branch mode

Set up for a busy counter or a stand, with one screening after another.

Only what is permitted is kept

Consent comes first, and the screening stores only the information the customer has agreed to.

The flow

Five short steps.

  1. Start

    The operator starts a quick screening.

  2. Consent

    The customer reads and agrees to what will be captured and kept.

  3. Capture

    The supported views are captured with on-screen guidance.

  4. Results

    A limited, educational summary is shown and explained.

  5. Next step

    Optional product information, or a referral to a clinician.

Screening, not diagnosis. Retail screening is educational and limited. It does not diagnose, prescribe or stand in for a clinician, and it does not imply a clinician has endorsed the result.
