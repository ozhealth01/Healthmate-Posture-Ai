Supported devices

Capture with what you already have.

Healthmate is designed to capture from everyday devices, with dedicated hardware to follow.

Phone

Smartphone camera.

Tablet

Tablet or iPad camera.

Computer

Laptop or desktop webcam.

Upload

Existing photos or video.

Different devices, different results. Capture channels are not interchangeable. Each has its own capture protocol and validation, and they should not be assumed to produce identical measurements. A dedicated Healthmate screening station and external hardware such as foot scanners and pressure platforms are planned for later.
