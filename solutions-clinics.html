A typical visit

Fits the consultation you already run.

  1. Select patient

    Open an existing record or create a new one, and confirm consent.

  2. Capture

    Follow the on-screen guide to capture the views the assessment needs.

  3. Review the draft

    Check the overlays and draft observations against what you see in front of you.

  4. Edit and approve

    Adjust wording, add your notes and approve the findings.

  5. Report and follow up

    Share the report and schedule the next comparison.

Observations, not diagnoses. Healthmate reports what can be observed and estimated from the capture, with stated limitations. Diagnosis and treatment decisions remain with the clinician.
