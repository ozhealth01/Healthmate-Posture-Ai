Legal and safety

Privacy

How Healthmate is designed to handle personal and health information.

Draft outline, not a privacy policy. This page sets out design principles only. The privacy policy must be written and reviewed by a qualified legal adviser, taking account of the applicable law including Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act, before Healthmate handles real personal data.

Principles Healthmate is designed around

  • Explicit consent. Information is collected with the person's agreement, for a stated purpose.
  • Least-privilege access. People see only what their role requires.
  • Organisation separation. Each clinic, hospital or retailer's records are kept apart from every other organisation's.
  • Encryption. Health and identity data is protected in storage and in transit.
  • Audit trails. Access to and changes in patient records are recorded.
  • Retention and deletion. Information is kept only as long as needed, with controls to delete it.
  • Secure report sharing. Reports are shared only with those permitted to receive them.

TRIMAX staff and patient data

TRIMAX commercial staff work with aggregated, operational information. They do not automatically have access to identifiable clinical records.
