How it works

From capture to report, with a clinician in the loop.

The AI drafts. A clinician decides. Every result is labelled so it is clear whether it is a draft or approved.

  1. Capture

    Take guided front, back and side views with a phone, tablet or webcam, or upload photos or video.

  2. Quality check

    Healthmate checks the capture is suitable before analysing it, and asks for a retake when it is not.

  3. Review

    The clinician sees draft observations with overlays, confidence and stated limitations.

  4. Approve

    Findings are edited where needed and approved by the clinician. Nothing is final until then.

  5. Report

    A clear, branded report is shared with the patient, with progress tracked across visits.

Safeguards

Clear about what the AI does, and what it does not.

Healthmate is designed to support professional judgement, and to say so plainly when it is unsure. Read the clinical limitations.

Clinician review

In professional workflows, AI findings are drafts until a clinician reviews, edits and approves them.

Screening, not diagnosis

Retail and public screening is educational and limited. It does not diagnose or prescribe.

Limitations shown

Uncertainty, unsuitable capture conditions and insufficient data are surfaced, not hidden.

Consent first

Assessments begin with explicit consent, and patients can see the reports they are permitted to access.

Role-based access

Each organisation's records are kept separate, and staff see only what their role allows.

Clinical and commercial kept apart

Product suggestions are optional and labelled, and never presented as clinical findings.
