Frequently asked questions

About the product

Does Healthmate diagnose conditions?

No. Healthmate reports what can be observed and estimated from images, with stated limitations. Diagnosis and treatment decisions remain with a qualified healthcare professional.

Who reviews the AI's findings?

In clinics and hospitals, a clinician reviews, edits and approves every set of findings before it is shared with a patient. Until then it is labelled as a draft.

How is retail screening different from a clinical assessment?

Retail screening is a short, educational screening with limited results. It does not diagnose or prescribe, and it points customers to a clinician where that would help.

What devices can I use?

A smartphone, a tablet, a laptop or desktop webcam, or uploaded photos and video. Each capture method has its own protocol and they should not be assumed to give identical measurements.

What happens if the photo is not good enough?

Healthmate checks capture quality first. If the capture is unsuitable it asks for a retake and does not produce findings from it.

Can patients see their own results?

Yes. Patients can access the reports they have been permitted to see, with plain-language explanations and any guidance their clinician has approved.

Will product recommendations affect the findings?

No. Product information is optional, clearly labelled and shown separately from clinical findings.

Who can see patient information?

Only people authorised to. Each organisation's records are kept separate, staff see what their role allows, and TRIMAX commercial staff do not automatically see identifiable clinical data.
