Legal and safety

Consent

Every assessment and screening starts with consent. This page explains the principles Healthmate is designed around.

Draft outline. This page describes design intent. The final consent wording must be written and reviewed by a qualified legal adviser before Healthmate is used with real patients.

Consent comes first

No images or video are captured until the person being assessed has been told what will happen and has agreed to it.

What people are told

  • What will be captured: photos or video of their posture or lower limbs.
  • What it will be used for: an AI-assisted assessment or an educational screening.
  • Who will be able to see it.
  • What will be kept, and for how long.
  • That the result is not a diagnosis.

Consent is specific

Agreeing to an assessment is separate from agreeing to anything else, such as receiving product information or having results shared with another provider.

Consent can be changed

People can review the permissions they have given and ask for them to be withdrawn.

Screening keeps only what is permitted

In retail and event screening, only the information the person has agreed to is stored.
